2025-06-08:paper Abstract:Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have shown remarkable performance in understanding charts. However, we identify a fundamental perception bottleneck that limits their ability to correctly interpret chart elements. Our work systematically analyzes the perceptual limitations of VLMs in chart understanding tasks. We introduce new benchmarks to evaluate chart comprehension and propose preprocessing strategies to overcome these bottlenecks.[]
