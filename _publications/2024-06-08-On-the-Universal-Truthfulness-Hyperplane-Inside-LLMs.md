2024-06-08:paper Abstract:We empirically demonstrate that naturally trained large language models (LLMs) possess a hyperplane of universal truthfulness, where models tend to produce truthful responses for inputs that lie on one side of this hyperplane. Our hyperplane approximation technique enables systematic analysis of LLM truthfulness. This work provides insights into the mechanisms of LLM truthfulness and speech acts.
