2024-06-08:paper Abstract:We propose SynLogic, a method for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale that enables large language models to learn logical reasoning and beyond. Our approach generates synthetic reasoning problems with step-by-step reasoning traces that can be verified for correctness, making it suitable for training LLMs on reasoning tasks. The method addresses the data scarcity and noise problems in existing reasoning datasets.
